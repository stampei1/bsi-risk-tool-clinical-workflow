BSI Watch
Day—

Demo with retrospective, de-identified MSK allo-HCT data on a simulated unit · not for clinical use

About

Each new stool sample gives two scores: the risk of an E. coli and of an Enterococcus bloodstream infection in the next 14 days. Patients are sorted so the highest risk is on top.

Tiers and rates come from the retrospective MSK allo-HCT cohort: High = top 10% of stool-sample scores, Watch = next 20%. Rates are the share of stool samples in each tier that were followed by that infection within 14 days. Scores come from models that never saw the patient shown (cross-validation); the cut-offs were chosen on the same cohort and have not been validated prospectively. Patients are real de-identified timelines placed together on a simulated unit; bed numbers are made up.
