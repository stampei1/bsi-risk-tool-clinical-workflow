Demo with retrospective, de-identified MSK allo-HCT data on a simulated unit · research prototype, not for clinical use

How it works

An agent with EHR access does the legwork every morning. The care team sees one short brief and only the patients who need a look.

  1. 1
    Gather

    Pulls new stool 16S results, antibiotic administrations, blood counts, vitals and microbiology for every patient on the unit.

  2. 2
    Score

    Runs two models on each new stool sample: risk of an E. coli and of an Enterococcus bloodstream infection in the next 14 days.

  3. 3
    Triage

    Sorts patients into High, Watch and Low, and flags anyone whose stool result is out of date.

  4. 4
    Brief

    Writes a two-line summary of what changed since yesterday and suggests next steps from the unit's protocol. Clinicians acknowledge or act.

What the tiers mean

Tiers are set on the retrospective MSK cohort: High = top 10% of stool-sample risk scores, Watch = next 20%. Rates are the share of samples in that tier followed by that infection within 14 days. Scores come from models that never saw the patient shown (cross-validation); the cut-offs and suggested steps are illustrative, not validated. Patients and bed numbers are real de-identified timelines placed on a simulated unit.
